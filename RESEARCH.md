# 🔬 Research & Innovation

## Current Research

### GHIRBAL Framework (غَرْبال)
**"Sifting Through Quality: An Integrated Framework for Arabic Translation Evaluation"**

#### 📚 Dissertation Title (Arabic):
*غَرْبال: دمج ضمان الجودة وضبطها والذكاء الاصطناعي في تقييم الترجمة إلى العربية*

#### Overview
GHIRBAL is the first comprehensive framework integrating Quality Assurance (QA), Quality Control (QC), and Artificial Intelligence for evaluating Arabic translation quality. The framework addresses the critical gap in translation evaluation methodologies specific to Arabic's unique linguistic characteristics.

#### Key Components

**1. Seven-Criterion Quality Model**
- ✅ Semantic accuracy
- ✅ Stylistic consistency
- ✅ Terminology compliance
- ✅ Grammatical correctness
- ✅ Cultural appropriateness
- ✅ Register alignment
- ✅ Format preservation

**2. Adaptive Footnote Policies**
- Contextual footnote generation
- Reader-level adaptation
- Genre-specific guidelines
- Cultural bridge building

**3. AI-Assisted Evaluation**
- Machine learning classification
- Pattern recognition in translation errors
- Automated quality scoring
- Non-translational failure detection

**4. Bias Screening Module**
- Cultural bias detection
- Gender representation analysis
- Ideological neutrality assessment
- Contextual appropriateness verification

#### Research Methodology
- **Sample Size:** 500+ professional translations
- **Languages:** Arabic, English, Italian
- **Domains:** Literary, Technical, Legal, Medical
- **Evaluation Period:** 2023-2026

#### Expected Outcomes
- Standardized Arabic translation quality metrics
- Automated QA/QC implementation guidelines
- AI training dataset for Arabic translation evaluation
- Industry best practices documentation
